About OrganLink

Our purpose, vision and objectives

What is OrganLink?

OrganLink is a web-based Smart Organ Donation Management System designed to organize organ donation-related registrations and information. It provides separate modules for donors and patients, hospital administration, patient updates, location services and awareness campaigns.


Our Mission

To improve access to organ donation information and support communication between registered users and authorized hospitals through a centralized digital platform.


Our Objectives

  • Make donor and patient registration easier.
  • Provide a hospital-managed donor–patient connection workflow.
  • Share hospital-approved updates with patients.
  • Promote awareness through nearby campaigns.
  • Support regional languages and location-based services.
  • Protect personal information through appropriate access controls in a future secure deployment.
