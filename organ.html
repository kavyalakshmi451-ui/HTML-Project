About LifeLink

We connect donors, patients and hospitals on one transparent platform so that no organ is wasted and no patient waits alone.

❤️
🎯 Mission

Make organ donation simple, safe and fair for everyone.

👁️ Vision

🔒 Values

Consent first, privacy always, and fair allocation by medical need.

Myths vs Facts

Myth: Doctors will not try to save me if I am a donor.
Fact: Saving your life always comes first. Donation is considered only after death is confirmed.
Myth: I am too old to donate.
Fact: There is no strict age limit. Doctors decide based on organ health.
Myth: Donation disfigures the body.
Fact: Surgery is done with care, and funeral arrangements are not affected.
